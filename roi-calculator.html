Assumptions Legend (Payer Deck Format)
Cohort Model: New ASD members are added each year and tracked by cohort. Program A costs stack until ABA duration ends, then that cohort ages out. Costs compound year-over-year with no natural endpoint.
Program B Completion: BrainyAct episode cost is time-limited (months). After completion, a shrinking percentage of graduates continue ABA at step-down rates (adjustable per year Y1–Y5). Costs decline as more members graduate and step down.
Financial Outputs: Net savings = Program A cost − Program B cost. ROI per $1 = 5-year net savings ÷ Program B 5-year cost. 10% annual churn applied to both programs. Severity mix and level-specific costs drive the weighted average.
